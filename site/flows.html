Flows of Empire

How much of a colony's trade ran through its metropole, and how quickly that tie loosened after independence. Built from RICardo (1800–1826), TRADHIST (1827–2014) and CEPII Gravity (2015–2020). All values are approximate and in current prices.

01 · THE EROSION CURVE

Median share of each former holding's recorded trade that went to or came from its former metropole, by years since independence. Lines appear where at least three holdings have data. Head, Mayer & Ries (2010) estimate that trade with the former coloniser falls by about 65% within four decades.

Inherited Systems

One file per country: where its first post-independence economic system came from. Each file weighs five influences (the former coloniser, the Soviet bloc, the United States with the World Bank and IMF, regional neighbours, and homegrown ideas) and lists the evidence. The shares are the project's judgement from the cited sources, not measurements.

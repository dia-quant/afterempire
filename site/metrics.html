Divergence Metrics

How closely each former colony's economic system resembled its former coloniser's, decade by decade, and when it turned towards the Soviet or the US / Bretton Woods model instead. Similarity is the average of up to five dimensions: planning regime, planning institution, monetary arrangement, legal origin and UN voting agreement. These figures describe resemblance; they do not establish cause.

SHEET 03Persistenceyears from independence to the first decade that departs from the coloniser

A dimension "departs" when its similarity to the former coloniser falls below 0.75: for example, leaving the coloniser's currency zone, or adopting a planning regime two steps away from the coloniser's. Decade resolution: a value of 0 means the first full decade after independence had already departed.

SHEET 04Trade ties and institutional ties

Each point is one curated country in one decade: the share of its recorded trade with the former metropole (from Flows of Empire) against its inheritance index. Countries that kept their trade tied to the metropole tended to keep more of its system, but this pattern alone does not tell which way the influence ran.

Codings and weights are documented in the pipeline (pipeline/build_systems.py) and the review sheet. Openness and government share are kept as context in each file on Inherited Systems, not in the similarity score.

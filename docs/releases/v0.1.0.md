First public release of **Afterempire**, a time-indexed atlas of the British, French, Portuguese and Dutch empires, 1800–2025.

**Website:** https://dia-quant.github.io/afterempire/

## What's in this release
- **Holdings gazetteer:** 177 holdings and 638 control periods, from colonies and protectorates to mandates, company rule and wartime occupations, cross-checked against COLDAT (`data/curated/holdings.csv`, `periods.csv`).
- **Trade flows, 1800–2020:** bilateral trade touching each holding, harmonised from RICardo (1800–1826), CEPII TRADHIST (1827–2014) and CEPII Gravity (2015–2020), with each holding's share of trade with its (former) metropole.
- **Development systems:** planning regime, planning institution and monetary arrangement by decade for 21 former colonies, with qualitative lineages and evidence notes; UN voting alignment for 94 ex-colonies; inheritance index and nearest model (coloniser, Soviet, US/Bretton Woods). Downloadable panel: `site/data/systems_panel.csv`.
- **Website:** seven pages (Territorial Ledger, Flows of Empire, Inherited Systems, Divergence Metrics, The Archive, Terms of Account, Correspondence) and the Python pipeline that builds them.

## Status
The development codings are drafts and have **not yet been reviewed**; review status is recorded row by row in `data/curated/systems_codings.csv`. Later versions will carry reviewed codings.
